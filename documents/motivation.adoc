= Motivation

To make pgdog rollouts smooth.

Pgdog's shutdown timer is 180s. If the clients all disconnect and reconnect themselves within 120s,
they are drained from pgdog before the pod terminates, and have already been routed to a fresher pgdog.

Persistent connections also keep load from ever balancing across all the dogs. Recycling connections
now and then lets the load eventually balance.

== Problem

When pgdog pods cycle in Kubernetes, Sequel can get bad disconnects in the middle of transactions.
